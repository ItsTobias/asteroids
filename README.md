My Asteroids Game!

- Shoot asteroids to make them split into smaller ones. If an asteroid is already at its smallest stage, shooting it will destroy it completely.
- Every 10 seconds (configurable in constants.py), a Golden Asteroid spawns. Golden Asteroids are much smaller and faster than normal asteroids, but they reward you with a large amount of points!
- A Super Shot is larger than a normal shot and, unlike regular shots, does not disappear after colliding with an asteroid. Use it wisely!
- The Random Teleport ability teleports you to a random location on the screen where there are no asteroids. However, you might get unlucky and teleport very close to an asteroid, so be careful and don't use it too often!

Keybinds (all are configurable):

Move — Arrow Keys
Boost — Left Shift
Shoot — Space
Super Shot — E
Random Teleport — R

Almost everything is configurable! Check out constants.py to customize the game to your liking.
